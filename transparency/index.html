LUIZ FERNANDO TOLEDO

Freedom of information

Luiz Fernando Toledo is a Brazilian investigative journalist who uses Brazil’s freedom of information law (Lei de Acesso à Informação, LAI) to obtain documents and examine public spending, public services and labour rights. His work includes presidential spending records, research on access to military documents and training for journalists.

REPORTING

What the requests revealed

Public records support reporting on how public money is spent and how people are treated. These examples show the documents obtained, the findings and, where documented, the public response.

Child protection and access to shelters

A TV Globo investigation used FOI responses to identify at least 16 children’s shelters closed over serious irregularities in São Paulo in four years. Reporting also showed children sleeping on the floor while waiting for placements. A subsequent lawsuit by prosecutors led to a court order requiring the city to identify a shelter place within one hour.

Reporting and the court order · G1 / TV Globo, 2019

Presidential spending

Requests and appeals obtained previously secret presidential corporate-card invoices. The records were organised for use by different newsrooms, allowing journalists and the public to scrutinise expenses and compare administrations.

How the invoices were obtained · Fiquem Sabendo

Forced labour and public works

Documents obtained from Brazil’s Chamber of Deputies through the FOI law helped identify requests to release parliamentary funding. Combined with labour-inspection records and a municipal contract, they traced links between a quarry where forced labour was identified and a publicly funded construction project.

The investigation · BBC News Brasil, 2025

Brazilian workers in Ireland

Diplomatic cables obtained through the FOI law recorded concerns about Brazilian meatpacking workers. Luiz Fernando Toledo and Laís Alegretti combined these documents with three months of interviews to investigate allegations of abuse and precarious conditions.

The investigation · BBC News Brasil, 2025

CAREER

Projects, research and journalism

Selected work related to freedom of information and the release of public data.

2017

Investigation exposed an effort to obstruct information requests

An audio recording obtained by O Estado de S. Paulo showed members of João Doria's administration discussing how to make responses more difficult and monitoring journalists who used the FOI law. The story led to the dismissal of the official quoted in the recording and became part of the debate over requester identity protection.

2019

Fiquem Sabendo

He cofounded Fiquem Sabendo, an independent news agency specialising in freedom of information. Its records and datasets contributed to thousands of stories published by different newsrooms.

Profile published by CGUICFJ impact report

2021

Research on practical barriers to disclosure

For his master's degree in Public Administration and Government at FGV EAESP, he analysed hundreds of thousands of declassified military documents. The study found that formal declassification did not guarantee practical access: operational barriers prevented access even to a small sample.

Original dissertationResearch summary

2022

Presidential corporate cards

He coordinated the project that obtained and organised invoices for previously secret presidential expenses. The dataset made it possible to compare administrations, verify expenditure and support investigations across different newsrooms.
