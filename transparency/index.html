CAREER

Projects, research and journalism

Selected work related to freedom of information and the release of public data.

2017

Reporting documented retaliation against requesters

An audio recording obtained by O Estado de S. Paulo showed João Doria's administration discussing how to make responses more difficult and monitoring the authors of requests. The case became a reference in the debate over identity protection. Federal regulations later ensured that recipient agencies cannot see who filed a request.

Original investigationCambridge: documented impact

2019

Fiquem Sabendo

He cofounded Brazil's first independent news agency specialising in freedom of information. Its records and datasets contributed to thousands of stories published by different newsrooms.

Profile published by CGUICFJ impact report

2021

Research on practical barriers to disclosure

For his master's degree in Public Administration and Government at FGV EAESP, he analysed hundreds of thousands of declassified military documents. The study found that formal declassification did not guarantee practical access: operational barriers prevented access even to a small sample.

Original dissertationResearch summary

2022

Presidential corporate cards

He coordinated the project that obtained and organised invoices for previously secret presidential expenses. The dataset made it possible to compare administrations, verify expenditure and support investigations across different newsrooms.

How the reporting was conductedO Assunto interview

2022–present

Data Fixers

He founded an organisation that obtains, cleans and connects public data for national and cross-border investigations. The project began at the Brown Institute for Media Innovation, a joint initiative of Columbia and Stanford universities.

RESULTS

Documented contributions

01

Protection for requesters

The reporting on the Doria administration documented the risk of retaliation when an agency knows a requester's identity. Identity protection was later incorporated into federal regulations.

02

Reusable data

Fiquem Sabendo submitted to CGU a public list of problems and proposed improvements for the migration to Fala.BR, covering search, filters, open files, attachments and clear protocol identification.
