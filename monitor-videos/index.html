How to read this: Counts are public platform totals at the time shown. A change is the difference between comparable snapshots, not an exact count of activity that day. Changes are blank when the data source changes. Views are plays, not unique people; the same viewer may be counted more than once or on different platforms. Missing figures are left blank. Instagram uses its public play counter, which can differ from older view counters. If a later reading falls below an earlier one, the highest observed count is retained and its verification time is shown.
